Data Deletion

Effective 20 October 2026 · for Animation Lab Uploader and the Animation Lab channels

Animation Lab Uploader only connects to the channel owner's own YouTube channels, Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts and Threads profiles. It holds no data about anyone else. If you are not the channel owner, the app has no data about you to delete. You can still email us any time and we'll confirm it.

1. Remove the app's access

Removing access stops the app's tokens working at once:

  • Facebook: open Facebook's Business integrations settings, or Apps and websites (Settings & privacy → Settings → Your activity and permissions). Select Animation Lab Uploader → Remove.
  • Instagram: Instagram → Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites → remove Animation Lab Uploader.
  • Threads: Threads → Settings → Account → Website permissions → remove Animation Lab Uploader.
  • Google / YouTube:
    1. Open myaccount.google.com/permissions (Third-party apps & services).
    2. For a brand channel, first switch to that channel's Brand Account with the account picker at the top right.
    3. Select Animation Lab Uploader → Delete all connections you have with Animation Lab Uploader.

2. Ask us to delete your data

  1. Email pyrahulpndt@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request", and give the account (username or Page) it concerns.
  2. We delete any access tokens the app holds for that account from our devices, and remove any app record that mentions it from our project files.
  3. We do this as soon as possible, and within 7 days, then confirm by email.

If you only revoke access in step 1, we also delete the stored token and any data received from that account within 7 days.

3. What gets deleted

  • access tokens and account IDs stored by the app;
  • any app record that mentions your account.

Videos and posts published on our own channels are our content, and they stay on the platforms unless we remove them there.
