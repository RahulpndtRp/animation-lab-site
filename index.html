About Animation Lab Uploader

Animation Lab Uploader is the private publishing tool behind these channels.

  • What it does: it uploads our own finished videos, with their titles, descriptions and cover images, to our own YouTube channels, Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts and Threads profiles. It uses the YouTube Data API, the Meta Graph API and the Threads API.
  • After each upload: it checks that the post went live and records its link.
  • Who uses it: only the channel owner, on their own computer. It isn't offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in.
  • Why it asks for Google access: it signs in to each of our YouTube channels and asks for two permissions:
    • youtube.upload, to upload our videos and set their thumbnails;
    • youtube.readonly, to check that the right channel is signed in and that each upload has finished processing.
    It reads no other YouTube data.
  • Why it asks for Meta access: to find our own Facebook Pages, their Instagram accounts and our Threads profiles, and to post our Reels and videos there.

It doesn't read messages, and it collects nothing about the people who watch our videos. Details: privacy policy · terms · data deletion.
